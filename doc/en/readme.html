Quick Research

Quick Research is an NVDA add-on that lets you look up selected text or a word at the cursor without leaving the application you are using. Results are presented in a navigable window or spoken, depending on your settings.

Getting started

Select text (or place the cursor on a word) and press NVDA+Alt+R. The Quick Research dialog opens with the text filled in and a list of research sources. Choose a source and press Enter to view the result.

Keyboard commands

  • NVDA+Alt+R: open the Quick Research dialog for selected text
  • NVDA+Alt+Shift+R: research selected text using the default source
  • NVDA+Alt+Ctrl+R: show the last research results again

Other source-specific commands can be assigned in NVDA's Input Gestures dialog, under the Quick Research category.

Built-in sources

Quick Research includes dictionary, Wikipedia, weather, local time, currency conversion, news, web search and map search sources. An internet connection is required for online lookups.

Currency conversion

Choose Currency converter to open a dedicated dialog. Enter an amount, select the From currency and To currency from the labelled dropdowns, and activate Convert. The default selections are US Dollar (USD) and Indian Rupee (INR).

Custom sources

Use Manage sources to add, edit, remove, import or export custom sources. Supported source types include opening a web address, RSS feeds, JSON data and readable web-page text.

Settings

Open NVDA settings and choose Quick Research. You can configure the language of spoken messages, result presentation, default source, Wikipedia and dictionary language, default city, home currency, temperature unit, headline count, network timeout and source visibility.

Privacy and network use

The text you research is sent to the service used by the selected source. Quick Research does not require an account or API key. Online sources require an internet connection. The add-on is disabled on secure screens.

Quick Research is an NVDA take on the JAWS Research It concept. The name of this add-on is Quick Research.
